**Deviations and limits.** The source is the AP Voter Poll, not VoteCast (ERRATA, 2026-11-04). The page prints no overall row, so the national figure is the mean of five tables. The rule's fallback applied for 2022: no same-source table exists, so the baseline is Pew's validated voters, on the 9 groups with both parties printed, with the 2022 national count as its overall (R +2.8; the shares 47.8 and 50.6 are from memory, the margin is from AP-NORC's assessment). Pew's independents exclude leaners and its definitions differ from the survey's. These numbers come from the Sep 11 run, whose prompt carried the date effect found on Nov 5. In the lock-date read, group error barely changed across dates (3.40 to 3.61 on the early table), so this result does not depend much on that run. One election is one data point, and a survey is not a count.
