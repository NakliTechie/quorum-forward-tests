## What this changes

- **The engine hit by standing still.** Approval came down 3 points to where the engine sits, so every core claim hit and
  B beat the poll-only rules. The tracking measure failed on all four arms. One wave like this does not show skill.
- **B's season deficit narrowed.** Twelve waves in, B's mean topline error fell from 3.09 to 2.96. The simple rules' means
  rose (Z1 2.00 → 2.08, Z2 1.64 → 1.75, Z3 1.77 → 1.85), and they still lead.
- **Republican approval fell toward the engine again.** B's Republican miss shrank from −17.1 to −10.7 because
  Republican approval fell 6, not because the engine moved.
- **The ballot arms cannot be read as intention forecasts this week.** The poll asked how people voted. F2's margin hit
  and its D-share miss say more about the gap between pre-election intention and recalled vote than about the engine.
- **The direction arm passed when direction printed 22.** It has passed five of seven public tests (FT6–FT12); both
  misses came when direction printed 29 or 30 (FT8, FT11).
