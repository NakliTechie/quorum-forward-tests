## What this means, and what it does not

- **The second scored wave at the second polling house.** HH1 scored 5 of 8 in September; HH2, which dropped the
  house offset, scores 7 of 10. The calibrated method, unchanged since the E/YouGov series and HH1-B, lands
  approval within 2.2 and the party cuts within 7 for the second month running (HH1-B: 0.0 and MAE 1.47). Two waves
  is still two waves.
- **The net is the weak quantity of the calibrated arm.** In both months its approval sat near the poll while its
  disapproval sat under it, because the engine's don't-know share runs above Harvard-Harris's (6.4 against 3).
  A don't-know repair for approval, like direction_dk's, is not registered for HH; adding one would be a new
  registration.
- **The ballot base changed under the forecast.** HH2 forecast the registered-voter frame. This month's deck prints
  the generic ballot among likely voters (52 / 48) and labels September "51%", where HH1 recorded 50 from
  September's own deck. Scored as printed, disclosed here; the persistence benchmark keeps its registered 50.
- **direction_dk's shrink factor was solved on September's don't-know (10%)**, disclosed at registration; October
  also printed 10, so its don't-know is in-sample. Its right-track level is not.
- No arm here is evidence of tracking: every forecast was fixed on Oct 6, before the poll moved 3 points on
  approval and 4 on right track.
