## What this means, and what it does not

- **Both forecasts said Americans were far more satisfied than they are.** The unadjusted
  forecast missed by 15 points. The adjusted one missed by 28. Both were well outside the
  5-point target.
- **The adjustment made it worse.** It was fitted on a single Pew survey from February 2025,
  a different survey, by web rather than phone, 19 months earlier. The registration named
  this as its main untested risk (disclosures 2 and 5). Satisfaction has been between 21% and
  27% for most of 2026, well below early 2025.
- **This is the first miss outside presidential approval.** The same method, unchanged, hit
  Harvard-Harris approval exactly (42.0 against 42, verdict 2026-10-01). Satisfaction did not
  transfer. A new question type needs its own check before any claim is made for it.
- **One poll, n = 1.** It does not show the method can never work for this question. It does
  show the registered adjustment for this question is wrong today.
