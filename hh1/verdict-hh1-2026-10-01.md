## What this means, and what it does not

- **The second polling house, first scored wave.** The FT-series calibrated method,
  unchanged (Platt `approval_pooled_2019_2025`, 3.6133 / 1.3759), landed Harvard-Harris's
  approval at 42.0 against 42, with party cuts within 2 points each. That is the method the
  E/YouGov series runs, transferred to another house's wording and frame with no refit.
  It is one wave, n = 1.
- **The registered HH1 arms were the weaker arms, and they scored 5 of 8.** HH1-B was
  registered after the August shadow exposed their defects; its repairs are frozen methods
  from elsewhere in the stack, and HH1-B is reported as a companion, not as HH1's result.
- **direction_dk's shrink factor was solved on the August wave's DK** (disclosed at
  registration): its DK of 10.9 against a published 10 is in-sample for that number.
- **The house offset did not earn its place.** Applied on top of the calibration it moved
  approval 7 points too high; applied to the raw topline (HH1) it hit the topline and broke
  the net.
- Neither engine arm is evidence of tracking: August to September moved 2 points on
  approval and right track, and every forecast here was fixed in August.
